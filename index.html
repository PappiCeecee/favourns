To Nyiko Favour Shivambo

I don't know if there's a right way to say this, so I'll just say it plainly. I miss you. Not in the small, passing way — the kind that sits quietly with me, in songs that suddenly mean more than they used to, in ordinary moments that aren't ordinary anymore because you're not in them.

Some people leave a mark and call it a memory. You left a light, and it's still on.

I keep coming back to our memories — our hangouts, those easy talks that never needed a reason, those dumb conversations that made no sense to anyone but us. They felt small at the time, but they meant so much to me. You made the ordinary feel easy.

Wherever you are tonight, I hope it's soft, and warm, and easy. And I hope some small part of this reaches you — carried on this song, on these little hearts, on everything I didn't say enough when I had the chance.

— always, however far
